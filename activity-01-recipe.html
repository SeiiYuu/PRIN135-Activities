Classic Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

A quick weeknight dinner that tastes like it took hours

Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 20 minutes
Servings 4 people
Difficulty Easy

About This Recipe

This garlic butter shrimp pasta comes together in under thirty minutes using one pan and pantry staples. It is rich, garlicky, and finished with a squeeze of lemon to keep every bite bright instead of heavy.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340g) spaghetti or linguine
  • 1 lb (450g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine or chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, juiced and zested
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Grated parmesan cheese, for serving

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package directions. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. While the pasta cooks, pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook for 1–2 minutes per side, until pink. Remove and set aside.
  4. Lower the heat to medium, add the remaining butter, garlic, and red pepper flakes. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Pour in the wine (or broth) and simmer for 2 minutes to reduce slightly.
  6. Add the drained pasta to the skillet along with a splash of reserved pasta water. Toss to coat, adding more pasta water as needed for a silky sauce.
  7. Return the shrimp to the pan, then stir in the lemon juice, lemon zest, and parsley.
  8. Taste and adjust seasoning, then serve immediately with grated parmesan on top.
Chef's Tip: Do not overcook the shrimp — they only need a couple of minutes per side. Pulling them out early and finishing them in the sauce keeps them tender instead of rubbery.

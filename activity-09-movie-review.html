Nebula Drift

A quiet science-fiction epic about what we carry across the stars.

Runtime2h 18m
ReleaseJuly 2026

Review

Nebula Drift tells the story of a salvage crew slowly discovering that the ship they've been stripping for parts still remembers its original passengers. Rather than lean into spectacle, director Elena Marsh keeps the camera close and patient, letting silence do as much work as the dialogue. It's a film more interested in grief than in gravity.

The pacing will test viewers expecting a conventional space thriller, but for those willing to sit with it, the final thirty minutes land with genuine emotional weight.

"The quietest science-fiction film of the year is also its most affecting."

Pros & Cons

What Works

  • Restrained, atmospheric cinematography
  • Strong lead performance
  • Sound design that builds real tension

What Doesn't

  • Slow first act may lose some viewers
  • Underdeveloped side characters
  • A couple of pacing lulls mid-film
